Three ways people love

Most people fall into one of three patterns. None of them is wrong, and none is fixed. About one in four people shift over a few years.

Secure

Comfortable being close. Says what they need, doesn't play games, and doesn't panic over small things.

Anxious

Loves deeply and notices every small change. Worries the other person will pull away, and can't settle until reassured.

Avoidant

Values independence. Wants love, but feels crowded when things get close, and pulls back.
